Room Planning Guides

Furniture sizes, the space to leave around them, and how to measure a room, in centimetres and inches.

Almost every "it does not fit" comes down to a number nobody wrote down: the doorway, the depth of the sofa, the room a chair needs to pull out. These guides collect the numbers worth knowing, as common rules of thumb rather than laws, and say how to check them in your own room.

Each one ends with how to try it in Scooch, the iPhone app I make for exactly this. Where a tape measure and a pencil are enough, the guide says so.

Common questions

How big is a standard 3-seater sofa?

Usually 190 to 230 cm (75 to 91 in) wide, 85 to 100 cm (33 to 39 in) deep and 75 to 90 cm (30 to 35 in) tall. Check the listing: sofas with the same number of seats vary a lot. Read more

How do I know if a sofa will fit through my door?

Compare the doorway's open width with the sofa's depth and height. If both are bigger, compare the diagonal depth: if that is smaller than the doorway, the sofa can be tipped through. Read more

How much space should be between a sofa and a coffee table?

35 to 45 cm (14 to 18 in): close enough to reach, far enough for your legs. Read more

How much space do you need around a bed?

At least 60 cm (24 in) on the sides you use and at the foot, and 75 to 90 cm (30 to 35 in) if you can. Read more

How much space do you need around a dining table?

75 to 90 cm (30 to 35 in) from the table's edge to a wall, so a chair can be pulled out. 90 to 120 cm (35 to 47 in) where people walk behind someone seated. Read more
