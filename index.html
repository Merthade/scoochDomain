Frequently asked questions

Is Scooch free?

Scooch is free to download, and your first room is on the house: draw it and see what fits. Keeping going is a one-time unlock, not a subscription. The price is shown in the app before you buy. If you had Scooch before version 1.0.1, everything stays unlocked for you. Read more

Can I type exact sizes from a shop listing?

Yes. Tap any piece of furniture or any room and type the numbers. Typed sizes stay exact, and the drawing redraws to match: more cushions on a wider sofa, more plates on a longer table.

Does it work in inches?

Yes. Switch between metric and imperial in Settings, and every measurement follows.

Can it tell me if a sofa fits through my door?

The app plans the room. For the doorway, use the free sofa fit calculator on this site: it checks the opening, tipping the sofa back, and a turn in the hallway. Read more

Is there a corner sofa?

Yes, as one L-shaped piece. Type the depth of each side on its own, mirror it to swap the side, and turn on the sofa bed to see where it pulls out. Read more

Where are my plans stored?

On your iPhone. If iCloud is available, Scooch also keeps a backup in your own iCloud, so a new phone can pick up where you left off. There is no account, and nothing is sent to me. Read more

Can I share or print a plan?

Yes. Export any plan as a PNG image or a PDF page and share it like any other file.

Does Scooch have ads, or need an account?

No ads and no account. Get the app

Is there an Android version?

Not at the moment: Scooch is made for iPhone.
