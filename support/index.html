Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Every email lands in a real inbox, and I read everything.

Email: emils@ozols.dev. I usually reply within a couple of days. Scooch is made by one person, so a short description of what you expected and what happened instead helps a lot. You can also use the feedback form inside the app.

Frequently asked

Is Scooch free?

Scooch is free to download. You can draw your first room and check the fit; keeping going is a one-time unlock, not a subscription. If you had Scooch before version 1.0.1, everything stays unlocked for you.

I bought it on another phone

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore on the unlock screen. The purchase comes back at no cost.

How do I get exact sizes?

Tap any piece of furniture or room and type the numbers. Typed dimensions stay exact. You can switch between metric and imperial in Settings.

Where are my plans stored?

On your device. If iCloud is available, Scooch also keeps a backup in your own iCloud storage so a new phone can pick up where you left off. Nothing is sent to me or anyone else.

Can I share a plan?

Yes. Export any plan as a PNG image or a PDF page from Settings and share it like any file.

Something is flagged red. Why?

Red means two things overlap in a way that would not work in real life, or a piece does not fit inside the room. Chairs tucked under tables are fine and stay unflagged.
