Scooch vs Home Design 3D

Home Design 3D is for designing a whole house over several floors, in 3D. Scooch is for checking that furniture fits a room before you buy or move it. Here is how the two compare, with every fact dated.

We make Scooch. Every fact about Home Design 3D comes from its US App Store listing and public US reviews, checked on 20 October 2026 (Home Design 3D version 5.10, Scooch version 1.0.2).

Pick Home Design 3D if you are planning a house or a renovation over several floors and want to see it in 3D. Pick Scooch if you have the room already and want to know whether the furniture fits.

Where Home Design 3D is stronger

  • It designs whole houses: several floors, angled walls, a 3D walkthrough and VR export.
  • Thousands of items against Scooch's 37 generic pieces.
  • You can import a blueprint and draw over it.
  • 18 languages against 16, and over 104,000 US ratings.

Where Scooch is stronger

  • It highlights furniture that overlaps or sticks out of the room; Home Design 3D's listing does not describe this.
  • $9.99 once instead of a subscription from $4.99 a week.
  • An 11 MB download against 987 MB.

What recent reviews of Home Design 3D mention

We read the 50 most recent US reviews of Home Design 3D, written between 10 June 2026 and 18 October 2026. 33 gave one or two stars and 14 gave four or five. Paraphrased:

  • The move to subscriptions is the most common complaint: many long-time users who bought the one-time Gold edition or content packs say they lost access after updates and are now asked to subscribe.
  • Several say most items and tools are locked, and that ads interrupt projects.
  • Several mention crashes after recent updates, including projects that no longer open.

The positive reviews praise it as an enjoyable way to design houses.

FAQ

Is Home Design 3D free?

Home Design 3D is a free download. Its US In-App Purchases list shows Weekly premium $4.99, monthly plans $12.99 to $22.99, and yearly plans from $44.99 to $79.99.

Does Home Design 3D warn you when furniture will not fit?

Its US App Store listing does not describe that, as of 20 October 2026. Scooch highlights any piece that overlaps another or sticks out of the room.

Is Scooch better than Home Design 3D?

It depends on the job. Pick Home Design 3D if you are planning a house or a renovation over several floors and want to see it in 3D. Pick Scooch if you have the room already and want to know whether the furniture fits.

Does Scooch do 3D or scan rooms?

No. Scooch is 2D: you measure the room and type or drag its size, then place furniture at real sizes.
