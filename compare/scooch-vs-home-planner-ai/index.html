Where Home Planner AI is stronger

It scans and measures

A built-in 3D scanner and a digital tape measure. With Scooch you measure the room yourself.

3D, renders and real products

Layouts in 2D and 3D with realistic renders, and brand-name furniture at its listed size. On the free tier, though, it offers up to 45 items per category.

Languages

30 languages, the most of any app compared here; Scooch has 16.

Where Scooch is stronger

A warning before you lift anything

Scooch outlines in red any piece that overlaps another or sticks out of the room, so a misfit shows before you buy. Home Planner AI's first screen promises 'real furniture that actually fits', but in our test, in its own furnished template room, it let us put the coffee table on the sofa without a word.

Typed sizes from the start

Rooms and furniture take the exact size you type. A recent Home Planner AI review says typing a room's size needed the subscription.

One clear price, after you have tried it

The guided first room is free, then $9.99 once. Home Planner AI shows its paywall before the first room, with weekly, monthly and 6-month plans, and its purchase list runs to ten items.

Room to open it, not just to stand it

Scooch draws each wardrobe door's swing, each drawer's pull-out and a sofa bed opened out, so you can see whether the door clears the desk and the drawer clears the bed, not only whether the piece fits.

About Scooch

A 2D room planner for checking that furniture fits, before anything heavy moves.

  • Rooms drawn at their real measurements, joined into an L or a U
  • 37 everyday pieces at real sizes, each resized to the centimetre
  • A drawing tool for any other piece, at any size
  • Overlaps and pieces outside the room outlined in red
  • Wardrobe doors, drawers and sofa beds drawn with the space they need to open
  • PNG and PDF export, metric or imperial, 26 themes
  • iPhone and iPad, synced through your own iCloud

What we saw using Home Planner AI

On 23 October 2026, on an iPhone 11 Pro Max with iOS 27.0.1, on an EU App Store account.†

  • Its first onboarding card reads 'Real furniture that actually fits', with 564,000+ real products at true-to-life sizes.
  • As soon as we picked a room shape, before the first room, its paywall came up: weekly, monthly or 6-month plans, each with a crossed-out price several times higher. Its X closes it.
  • On the free tier its catalogue offers up to 45 items per category, and asks for the subscription for the full 564,000.
  • Selected furniture shows its distance to each wall.
  • From its own furnished 4.00 by 5.10 m living room template we moved the coffee table onto the corner sofa. Nothing was flagged.
  • Back on its home screen after closing the paywall: 'Price Crash! HUGE DISCOUNT for all PRO subscriptions' with a one-hour countdown, and 'Add Living room (9 left)'.

What Scooch does not do

  • Scan a room. You measure it and type the size; a tape measure is all it takes.
  • Show it in 3D or render it. Scooch is 2D.
  • Sell brand furniture. Everyday pieces you resize to the centimetre, and a drawing tool for any piece, from its product page's measurements.

Questions

Can Scooch scan a room?

No. You measure the room and type or drag its size. Home Planner AI has a built-in 3D scanner.

Does Home Planner AI warn when furniture will not fit?

Not in our test (version 26.50, 23 October 2026): in its own furnished template room, a coffee table moved onto the sofa was not flagged. Scooch outlines both pieces in red.
