1. Scooch (ours)

by Emils Ozols · Best for: Checking that furniture fits a room before you buy or move it

A 2D planner built around one question: will it fit? You type or drag your rooms to their real measurements, drop in furniture at real sizes, and anything that overlaps or sticks out of the room is highlighted before you lift it.

Pros

  • Flags furniture that overlaps another piece or sticks out of the room
  • Every piece resizes to the centimetre or inch you type, and you can draw your own
  • One-time $9.99, no subscription and no account
  • 11 MB download; iPhone and iPad, synced through your own iCloud
  • 26 colour themes and 16 languages

Cons

  • No 3D view, no renders, no camera or LiDAR scan: you measure and type
  • Rooms are rectangles; L and U shapes are rooms joined together, no angled walls
  • 37 generic pieces: no brand catalogue, materials or decor
  • Needs iOS 26 and an iPhone or iPad: no Mac, Android or web
  • Planning needs the $9.99 unlock; only 2 US ratings so far

Price (US): Free download. After the short intro, planning needs the one-time unlock: $9.99 (US). No subscription, no account. The unlock works on your iPhone and iPad with the same Apple Account.

FAQ

Which room planner apps are free?

All nine are free to download, but each charges for the full app: Scooch and Draft Paper $9.99 once, Keyplan 3D Lite $5.99 once for its Standard version, and the others by subscription (Live Home 3D also sells lifetime upgrades). The free versions differ: see each app's price above.

Which app tells me if my furniture will fit?

Scooch highlights furniture that overlaps another piece or sticks out of the room. As of 20 October 2026, none of the other eight apps' US App Store listings describe a check like that.

Do I need a 3D app to plan furniture?

Not to check fit: a plan drawn to scale answers whether a piece fits. 3D helps when you want to see the style of a room; Planner 5D, Home Planner AI, Home Design 3D, Keyplan 3D Lite and Live Home 3D do that, Scooch does not.

Which apps can scan a room with the camera?

Per their listings: magicplan, Planner 5D, Home Planner AI and Live Home 3D (with LiDAR). Scooch cannot: you measure the room and type its size.
