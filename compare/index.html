Best Room Planner Apps for iPhone (2026): 9 Compared

There is no single best room planner, because people want different things from one. Some want to see a room in 3D, some need to scan it for work, some just need to know whether the sofa fits. Here are 9 iPhone room planners, what each does best, and where each falls short. One question runs through all of them, and we tested it in each app we could open: when a piece does not fit, does the app tell you?*

1. Scooch

By Emils Ozols · Best for: Checking that furniture fits a room before you buy or move it · 5.0★ average US rating

A 2D room planner built around one question: will it fit? You type or drag your rooms to their real measurements, place furniture at real sizes, and anything that overlaps another piece or sticks out of the room is outlined in red before you lift it. It also draws the space each wardrobe door, drawer and sofa bed needs to open.

Pros

  • Turns furniture red when it does not fit
  • Shows door swings and drawer pull-outs
  • Everyday pieces, or draw your own
  • $9.99 once, no account, no ads

Cons

  • Rectangular rooms only
  • 2D only, no 3D view
  • Needs iOS 26 or later

Questions

Which room planner app for iPhone is best?

It depends on the job. To check that furniture fits, Scooch (our app). For 3D design and renders, Planner 5D or Live Home 3D. To scan a room for work, magicplan. For whole houses over several floors, Home Design 3D.

Which room planner apps have no subscription?

From this list: Scooch and Draft Paper ($9.99 once) and Keyplan 3D Lite ($5.99 once for its Standard version). Live Home 3D sells lifetime upgrades as well as subscriptions.

Which app tells me if my furniture will fit?

Scooch outlines in red any piece that overlaps another or sticks out of the room. On 23 October 2026 we took a furnished room in five of the others (Home Design 3D, Planner 5D, magicplan, Live Home 3D and Home Planner AI), moved the coffee table onto the sofa, and none of them flagged it; none of the other listings describe a check like that.

How were these apps chosen?

From the top results for room and floor planner searches on the US App Store, keeping apps that plan rooms on iPhone and have an active listing. Facts come from each listing, purchase list, privacy label and recent public reviews, checked on 23 October 2026, and from using each app ourselves on an iPhone.
