1. Scooch

By Emils Ozols · Best for: Checking that furniture fits a room before you buy or move it · 5.0★ average US rating

A 2D room planner built around one question: will it fit? You type or drag your rooms to their real measurements, place furniture at real sizes, and anything that overlaps another piece or sticks out of the room is highlighted before you lift it.

Pros

  • Warns when furniture overlaps or sticks out of the room
  • Every piece resizes to the centimetre, and you can draw your own
  • $9.99 once: no subscription, no account
  • iPhone and iPad, synced through your own iCloud

Cons

  • Rooms are rectangles: no angled or curved walls
  • 37 generic pieces: no brand catalogue or decor
  • Needs iOS 26 or later

Questions

Which room planner app for iPhone is best?

It depends on the job. To check that furniture fits, Scooch (our app). For 3D design and renders, Planner 5D or Live Home 3D. To scan a room for work, magicplan. For whole houses over several floors, Home Design 3D.

Which room planner apps have no subscription?

From this list: Scooch and Draft Paper ($9.99 once) and Keyplan 3D Lite ($5.99 once for its Standard version). Live Home 3D sells lifetime upgrades as well as subscriptions.

Which app tells me if my furniture will fit?

Scooch highlights furniture that overlaps another piece or sticks out of the room. As of 20 October 2026, none of the other eight apps' US App Store listings describe a check like that.

How were these apps chosen?

From the top results for room and floor planner searches on the US App Store, keeping apps that plan rooms on iPhone and have an active listing. Facts come from each listing, purchase list, privacy label and recent public reviews, checked on 20 October 2026.
