Where DrawPlan is stronger

Tracing

Import a plan or photo and draw over it.

Drawing help

Free sketching or to-scale drawing, plus AI that helps lay out the plan.

Older iPhones and more languages

It runs on iOS 15 or later, in 38 languages; Scooch needs iOS 26 and has 16.

Where Scooch is stronger

It opens

Scooch runs on iOS 26 and iOS 27. On our iPhone with iOS 27.0.1, DrawPlan 6.0.1 closed itself at every launch, also when we opened it by hand.

A warning before you lift anything

Scooch outlines in red a piece that overlaps another or sticks out of the room. DrawPlan's listing does not describe a check like that.

Your plans stay yours

After the one-time unlock every plan opens, exports as PNG or PDF and syncs through your own iCloud. DrawPlan's listing puts sync and clean exports behind a subscription.

About Scooch

A 2D room planner for checking that furniture fits, before anything heavy moves.

  • Rooms drawn at their real measurements, joined into an L or a U
  • 37 everyday pieces at real sizes, each resized to the centimetre
  • A drawing tool for any other piece, at any size
  • Overlaps and pieces outside the room outlined in red
  • Wardrobe doors, drawers and sofa beds drawn with the space they need to open
  • PNG and PDF export, metric or imperial, 26 themes
  • iPhone and iPad, synced through your own iCloud

What Scooch does not do

  • Trace a plan or photo. You type or drag the room's measurements.
  • Sketch freehand. Rooms and furniture snap to real sizes.
  • Charge monthly to sync or export. $9.99 once covers every plan, PNG and PDF export and iCloud sync.

From the 100 most recent US App Store reviews of DrawPlan, read on 23 October 2026. These are individual experiences, not tests we ran, but these themes came up more than once:

Pricing

DrawPlan is free to download; its plans are $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year, Plus $4.99 or $49.99, and Pro $9.99 or $99.99, with AI credits from $4.99 to $19.99 (US). Scooch is a free download; planning needs a one-time $9.99 unlock.

Questions

Is DrawPlan free?

It is a free download; per its listing, cloud sync, high-resolution exports and exports without grids or watermarks need a subscription, from $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year (US).

Does DrawPlan work on iOS 27?

Not on our iPhone with iOS 27.0.1: version 6.0.1 closed itself at every launch (23 October 2026). Its crash report points at a check iOS 27 now enforces on how apps start.

Does DrawPlan warn when furniture will not fit?

Its listing does not describe a check like that, and we could not open it to test one. Scooch outlines a piece in red when it overlaps another or sticks out of the room.

Rooms at their real size, furniture at real sizes, and a red outline when something does not fit. $9.99 once.
