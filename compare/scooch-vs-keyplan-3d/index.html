We make Scooch. Every fact about Keyplan 3D Lite comes from its US App Store listing and public US reviews, checked on 20 October 2026 (Keyplan 3D Lite version 1.9.9, Scooch version 1.0.2).

Pick Keyplan 3D Lite if you want to build a home's shape freely and decorate it in 3D, at a low one-time price. Pick Scooch if you want exact furniture sizes and a warning when something does not fit.

Where Keyplan 3D Lite is stronger

  • Walls can take any shape; Scooch's rooms are rectangles you join.
  • It shows the plan in 3D and lets you paint and decorate surfaces.
  • Its one-time Standard upgrade is $5.99, cheaper than Scooch's $9.99.
  • Over 52,000 US ratings.

Where Scooch is stronger

  • It highlights furniture that overlaps or sticks out of the room; Keyplan's listing does not describe this.
  • Every piece resizes to the centimetre you type, and you can draw your own pieces.
  • 16 languages against 10, and an 11 MB download against 663 MB.

What recent reviews of Keyplan 3D Lite mention

We read the 50 most recent US reviews of Keyplan 3D Lite, written between 16 October 2025 and 17 October 2026. 10 gave one or two stars and 27 gave four or five. Paraphrased:

  • The limits of the Lite version are the most common complaint: several say more rooms, floors or paint textures need a purchase.
  • A few mention plans lost when moving to a new phone, or that sizing spaces precisely is hard.

Most of these reviews are positive, calling it easy to use and worth the money.

FAQ

Is Keyplan 3D Lite free?

Keyplan 3D Lite is a free download. Its US In-App Purchases list shows the Standard version at $5.99 and object packs from $1.99 to $2.99 (a $23.90 Starter Pack is also featured on the page).

Does Keyplan 3D Lite warn you when furniture will not fit?

Its US App Store listing does not describe that, as of 20 October 2026. Scooch highlights any piece that overlaps another or sticks out of the room.

Is Scooch better than Keyplan 3D Lite?

It depends on the job. Pick Keyplan 3D Lite if you want to build a home's shape freely and decorate it in 3D, at a low one-time price. Pick Scooch if you want exact furniture sizes and a warning when something does not fit.

Does Scooch do 3D or scan rooms?

No. Scooch is 2D: you measure the room and type or drag its size, then place furniture at real sizes.
