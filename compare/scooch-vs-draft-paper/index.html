Draft Paper alternative: Draft Paper vs Scooch

Draft Paper and Scooch cost the same, $9.99 once, and both draw to scale. Draft Paper draws anything; Scooch knows what a room and a sofa are.*

Pick Draft Paper if you want a general drafting tool with layers and DXF export, on an older iPhone too.
Pick Scooch if you want rooms and furniture that already know their sizes, and a warning when they do not fit.

Where Draft Paper is stronger

It draws anything

Lines, shapes, text and measurements on up to 10 layers, not only rooms and furniture.

CAD export

It exports DXF, so the drawing opens in CAD software.

Older iPhones

It runs on iOS 15 or later; Scooch needs iOS 26.

Where Scooch is stronger

Furniture, ready made

37 pieces at real sizes, each resized to the centimetre, instead of shapes you draw.

A warning before you lift anything

Scooch highlights a piece that overlaps another or sticks out of the room.

Languages

16 languages; Draft Paper's listing names English only.

About Scooch

A 2D room planner for checking that furniture fits, before anything heavy moves.

  • Rooms drawn at their real measurements, joined into an L or a U
  • 37 pieces at real sizes, each resized to the centimetre
  • Furniture you draw yourself, at any size
  • Overlaps and pieces outside the room highlighted
  • Door swings, drawers and sofa beds shown with the space they need
  • PNG and PDF export, metric or imperial, 26 themes
  • iPhone and iPad, synced through your own iCloud

See all features

What Scooch does not do

  • Rooms are rectangles: no angled or curved walls
  • 37 generic pieces: no brand catalogue or decor
  • Needs iOS 26 or later

Pricing

Draft Paper is free to download; All Features is $9.99 once (US). Scooch is a free download; planning needs a one-time $9.99 unlock. Neither has a subscription.

Questions

Do they cost the same?

Yes: both are $9.99 once in the US, with no subscription.

Does Draft Paper have furniture?

Its listing describes drawing tools (lines, rectangles, ellipses, text, measurements), not a furniture catalogue.

Can Scooch export DXF?

No. Scooch exports PNG and PDF.
