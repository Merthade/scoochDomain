Scooch vs Draft Paper

Draft Paper is for quick, precise 2D drawings and DXF export. Scooch is for checking that furniture fits a room before you buy or move it. Here is how the two compare, with every fact dated.

We make Scooch. Every fact about Draft Paper comes from its US App Store listing and public US reviews, checked on 20 October 2026 (Draft Paper version 1.19.6, Scooch version 1.0.2).

Pick Draft Paper if you want a general drafting tool with layers and DXF export. Pick Scooch if you want rooms and furniture that already know their sizes and warn you when they do not fit.

Where Draft Paper is stronger

  • It draws anything, not only rooms and furniture, on up to 10 layers.
  • It exports DXF, so the drawing opens in CAD software.
  • It runs on iOS 15 or later; Scooch needs iOS 26.
  • A 2 MB download, and over 15,000 US ratings.

Where Scooch is stronger

  • Furniture is ready made: 37 pieces at real sizes, resized to the centimetre, instead of shapes you draw.
  • It highlights furniture that overlaps or sticks out of the room.
  • 16 languages against English only.

What recent reviews of Draft Paper mention

We read the 100 most recent US reviews of Draft Paper, written between 23 August 2024 and 16 October 2026. 44 gave one or two stars and 40 gave four or five. Paraphrased:

  • Several long-time users say a recent update (1.19.5) changed scrolling and undo and made drawing harder.
  • Several mention crashes, some losing minutes of work, including with Apple Pencil on iPad.
  • Several could not switch between metric and imperial, or import an image.

The positive reviews call it simple, flexible and good enough for daily work.

FAQ

Is Draft Paper free?

Draft Paper is a free download. All Features: $9.99, one time (its US In-App Purchases list).

Does Draft Paper warn you when furniture will not fit?

Its US App Store listing does not describe that, as of 20 October 2026. Scooch highlights any piece that overlaps another or sticks out of the room.

Is Scooch better than Draft Paper?

It depends on the job. Pick Draft Paper if you want a general drafting tool with layers and DXF export. Pick Scooch if you want rooms and furniture that already know their sizes and warn you when they do not fit.

Does Scooch do 3D or scan rooms?

No. Scooch is 2D: you measure the room and type or drag its size, then place furniture at real sizes.
