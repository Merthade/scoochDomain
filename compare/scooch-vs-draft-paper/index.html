Pick Draft Paper if you want a general drafting tool with layers and DXF export, on an older iPhone too.
Pick Scooch if you want rooms and furniture that already know their sizes, and a warning when they do not fit.

Where Draft Paper is stronger

It draws anything

Lines, shapes, text and measurements on up to 10 layers, not only rooms and furniture.

CAD export

It exports DXF, so the drawing opens in CAD software.

Older iPhones

It runs on iOS 15 or later; Scooch needs iOS 26.

Where Scooch is stronger

Furniture, ready made

37 pieces at real sizes, each resized to the centimetre, instead of shapes you draw. In Draft Paper the sheet starts blank and every line is yours.

A warning before you lift anything

Scooch outlines in red a piece that overlaps another or sticks out of the room. A drawing app has no furniture to check.

Languages

16 languages; Draft Paper's listing names English only.

About Scooch

A 2D room planner for checking that furniture fits, before anything heavy moves.

  • Rooms drawn at their real measurements, joined into an L or a U
  • 37 everyday pieces at real sizes, each resized to the centimetre
  • A drawing tool for any other piece, at any size
  • Overlaps and pieces outside the room outlined in red
  • Wardrobe doors, drawers and sofa beds drawn with the space they need to open
  • PNG and PDF export, metric or imperial, 26 themes
  • iPhone and iPad, synced through your own iCloud

What we saw using Draft Paper

On 23 October 2026, on an iPhone 11 Pro Max with iOS 27.0.1, on an EU App Store account.†

  • A short tutorial, then a blank sheet of grid paper. No account, no prompts and no furniture: you draw every line yourself.
  • We drew a living room with its rectangle tool. Zoomed out, lines snap to whole metres; zoomed in, to 10 cm. Shapes that touch merge into one outline, so a sofa drawn against a wall turned into a notch in the wall.
  • Pages 3 to 6 carry a padlock; tapping one asks you to unlock every feature, once.

What Scooch does not do

  • Export DXF. PNG and PDF only.
  • Draw anything. Rooms and furniture only, which is what lets it warn you.
  • Run on older iPhones. It needs iOS 26, which every iPhone since the iPhone 11 (2019) can run.

What recent reviews of Draft Paper mention

From the 100 most recent US App Store reviews of Draft Paper, read on 23 October 2026. These are individual experiences, not tests we ran, but these themes came up more than once:

  • a recent update (1.19.5) that changed scrolling and undo and made drawing harder
  • crashes that lose minutes of work, including with Apple Pencil on iPad
  • trouble switching between metric and imperial, or importing an image

Pricing

Draft Paper is free to download, with pages 3 to 6 locked; All Features is $9.99 once (US). Scooch is a free download; planning needs a one-time $9.99 unlock. Neither has a subscription.

Questions

Do they cost the same?

Yes: both are $9.99 once in the US, with no subscription.

Does Draft Paper have furniture?

No. Its listing describes drawing tools (lines, rectangles, ellipses, text, measurements), and in our test the sheet starts blank: every shape is drawn by hand.

Can Scooch export DXF?

No. Scooch exports PNG and PDF.
