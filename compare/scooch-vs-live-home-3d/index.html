Where Live Home 3D is stronger

Full 3D design

Walkthroughs, renders, flyover videos, roofs, terrain and lighting.

Scanning

A LiDAR room scanner on devices that have the sensor; with Scooch you measure.

Catalogue and platforms

5,000+ items and 3,000+ materials, and versions for Mac and Windows.

Where Scooch is stronger

A warning before you lift anything

Scooch outlines in red a piece that overlaps another or sticks out of the room. In our test, in Live Home 3D's furnished studio sample, the corner sofa went over the coffee table and an armchair without a word.

Price

Everything in Scooch is $9.99 once; Live Home 3D's Standard upgrade is $24.99 a year or $59.99 lifetime, and its free version kept Save greyed out and edits one project at a time.

Languages

16 languages to Live Home 3D's 12.

Room to open it, not just to stand it

Scooch draws each wardrobe door's swing, each drawer's pull-out and a sofa bed opened out, so you can see whether the door clears the desk and the drawer clears the bed, not only whether the piece fits.

About Scooch

A 2D room planner for checking that furniture fits, before anything heavy moves.

  • Rooms drawn at their real measurements, joined into an L or a U
  • 37 everyday pieces at real sizes, each resized to the centimetre
  • A drawing tool for any other piece, at any size
  • Overlaps and pieces outside the room outlined in red
  • Wardrobe doors, drawers and sofa beds drawn with the space they need to open
  • PNG and PDF export, metric or imperial, 26 themes
  • iPhone and iPad, synced through your own iCloud

What we saw using Live Home 3D

On 23 October 2026, on an iPhone 11 Pro Max with iOS 27.0.1, on an EU App Store account.†

  • No account and no prompts: a welcome screen, then templates and sample rooms.
  • In the free version, Save was greyed out, and opening a second project warned that the first could no longer be edited: the free version handles one project at a time.
  • Its store sells Standard and Pro, each monthly, yearly or for life.
  • In its furnished studio sample we pushed the corner sofa over the coffee table and an armchair. Nothing was flagged. Earlier, a sofa dragged half through a wall went unflagged too.

What Scooch does not do

  • Do 3D, renders or video. Scooch is 2D, for checking what fits.
  • Scan with LiDAR. You measure the room and type its size.
  • Run on a Mac or Windows. Scooch is for iPhone and iPad, synced through your own iCloud.

Pricing

Live Home 3D has a free version that watermarks exports and keeps one project at a time; in our test its Save was greyed out. Standard is $6.99 a month, $24.99 a year or $59.99 lifetime; Pro is $14.99 a month, $49.99 a year or $129.99 lifetime (US). Scooch is a free download; planning needs a one-time $9.99 unlock.

Questions

Is Live Home 3D free?

There is a free version; per its listing, exports carry a watermark and only one project can be used at a time, and in our test Save was greyed out.

Can Scooch scan with LiDAR?

No. You measure the room and type or drag its size.

Does Live Home 3D warn when furniture will not fit?

Not in our test (version 5.1, 23 October 2026): in its furnished studio sample, the sofa pushed over the coffee table was not flagged. Scooch outlines both pieces in red.
