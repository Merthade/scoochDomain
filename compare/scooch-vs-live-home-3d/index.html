About Scooch

A 2D room planner for checking that furniture fits, before anything heavy moves.

  • Rooms drawn at their real measurements, joined into an L or a U
  • 37 pieces at real sizes, each resized to the centimetre
  • Furniture you draw yourself, at any size
  • Overlaps and pieces outside the room highlighted
  • Door swings, drawers and sofa beds shown with the space they need
  • PNG and PDF export, metric or imperial, 26 themes
  • iPhone and iPad, synced through your own iCloud

What Scooch does not do

  • Rooms are rectangles: no angled or curved walls
  • 37 generic pieces: no brand catalogue or decor
  • Needs iOS 26 or later

Pricing

Live Home 3D has a free version that watermarks exports and keeps one project at a time. Standard is $5.99 a month, $19.99 a year or $59.99 lifetime; Pro is $14.99 a month, $39.99 a year or $129.99 lifetime (US). Scooch is a free download; planning needs a one-time $9.99 unlock.

Questions

Is Live Home 3D free?

There is a free version; per its listing, exports carry a watermark and only one project can be used at a time.

Can Scooch scan with LiDAR?

No. You measure the room and type or drag its size.

Does Live Home 3D warn when furniture will not fit?

Its US App Store listing does not describe that, as of 20 October 2026. Scooch highlights any piece that overlaps another or sticks out of the room.
