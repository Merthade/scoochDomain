Where magicplan is stronger

It measures for you

magicplan builds the floor plan from the phone's camera. When it works, that is quick; recent reviews describe scans that miss closets, walls or doors. For the one room you are furnishing, a tape measure takes a few minutes, and you need it for the furniture anyway. With Scooch you measure the room and type its size.

Made for jobs

Photos, notes, forms, 360° panoramas, reports and estimates sit on the plan, and it connects to Xactimate and CoreLogic. That is what its prices pay for, from $129.99 a year. At home, with no one to bill, it is paperwork you will not use.

A long track record

Years on the App Store and far more ratings than Scooch, which is new. Its listing speaks to remodelers, restoration and claims professionals, and that is who its features and prices are built around.

Where Scooch is stronger

A warning before you lift anything

Scooch outlines in red a piece that overlaps another or sticks out of the room. In our test, in magicplan's furnished demo flat, the coffee table moved onto the sofa without a word.

Furniture that knows its size

37 pieces at real sizes, each resized to the centimetre, plus pieces you draw yourself.

No account, one price

$9.99 once, nothing to sign up for. magicplan's free Starter plan needs a sign-up and holds 2 projects; its paid plans start at $12.99 a month or $129.99 a year.

Languages

Scooch is in 16 languages; magicplan's listing names 5.

Room to open it, not just to stand it

Scooch draws each wardrobe door's swing, each drawer's pull-out and a sofa bed opened out, so you can see whether the door clears the desk and the drawer clears the bed, not only whether the piece fits.

About Scooch

A 2D room planner for checking that furniture fits, before anything heavy moves.

  • Rooms drawn at their real measurements, joined into an L or a U
  • 37 everyday pieces at real sizes, each resized to the centimetre
  • A drawing tool for any other piece, at any size
  • Overlaps and pieces outside the room outlined in red
  • Wardrobe doors, drawers and sofa beds drawn with the space they need to open
  • PNG and PDF export, metric or imperial, 26 themes
  • iPhone and iPad, synced through your own iCloud

What we saw using magicplan

On 23 October 2026, on an iPhone 11 Pro Max with iOS 27.0.1, on an EU App Store account.†

  • Its first screen asks whether you already have an account. As a new user we were offered the free Starter plan, all features for up to 2 projects, after a sign-up.
  • Without an account there is a demo that warns 'Changes will not be saved'. It opens a sample flat with every wall measured.
  • Selected furniture offers Set Distance, to place it a set distance from a wall.
  • In its furnished demo flat we moved the coffee table onto the sofa. Nothing was flagged. A couch inserted from its catalogue had also landed across a wall and inside a door's swing without a word.

What Scooch does not do

  • Scan a room. You measure it and type the size.
  • Write reports or estimates. Scooch is for furnishing your own rooms; magicplan is built for jobs.
  • Ask for an account. Plans stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

What recent reviews of magicplan mention

From the 100 most recent US App Store reviews of magicplan, read on 23 October 2026. These are individual experiences, not tests we ran, but these themes came up more than once:

  • core features, extra plans and scanning that need a subscription, with pricing some found unclear
  • scans that miss closets, walls or doors, or struggle with open-plan rooms and sloped ceilings
  • projects that did not save, or disappeared after an update or an account change
  • a steep learning curve for first-time users

Pricing

magicplan's free Starter plan needs a sign-up and holds up to 2 projects; without one, its demo is not saved. Its paid plans are Sketch at $12.99 a month or $129.99 a year, Report at $39.99 or $399.99, and Estimate at $89.99 or $899.99 (US). Scooch is a free download; planning needs a one-time $9.99 unlock, with no subscription.

Questions

Can Scooch scan a room like magicplan?

No. Scooch has no camera or LiDAR scan: you measure the room and type or drag its size.

Does magicplan warn when furniture will not fit?

Not in our test (version 2026.41.0, 23 October 2026): in its furnished demo flat, a coffee table moved onto the sofa was not flagged. Scooch outlines both pieces in red.

Which is cheaper?

Scooch: $9.99 once. magicplan's paid plans start at $12.99 a month or $129.99 a year; its free plan holds 2 projects.
